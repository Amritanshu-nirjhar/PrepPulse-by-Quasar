The idea

Most mess waste isn't carelessness. It's guesswork.

Kitchens over-cook because under-cooking feels riskier — running out looks worse than throwing out. PrepPulse removes the guesswork by turning every meal into a data point, and every data point into tomorrow's exact prep quantity.

Every plate served, every tray scraped into the bin, gets logged. Over weeks, PrepPulse builds a living model of how your specific mess actually eats — not a generic average, but your hostel's, your campus's, your city's own rhythm.

That model doesn't just look at headcount. It reads the whole context around a meal: what day it is, what's on the plate, what's happening on campus, and what the sky is doing outside.

The output is simple: a per-dish quantity for the next meal, in kilograms, with the reasoning attached — so the kitchen staff aren't just told a number, they understand why.

SEASON

HISTORY

Dish-level consumption trends

Some dishes are always finished, others always leave leftovers — PrepPulse learns each dish's own pattern.

The pipeline

From last night's leftovers to tomorrow's exact order

PrepPulse runs a five-stage loop, once per meal, every day.

01

Log

Kitchen staff weigh what's served and what's left over, per dish, in under a minute using the PrepPulse tablet app.

02

Learn

The model folds in the day type, weather, academic calendar and recent trend for every dish in the log.

03

Predict

PrepPulse forecasts tomorrow's likely diner count and, per dish, the exact quantity worth cooking.

04

Pulse

A prep sheet lands with the mess manager the night before — quantities, confidence level, and the reasoning.

05

Ask

Anything unusual coming up? The kitchen just asks the PrepPulse chatbot and gets an adjusted number instantly.

Ask PrepPulse

An AI chatbot that runs the numbers for you

It's built on the same forecasting model behind the dashboard — so an off-the-cuff question the night before a surprise event gets the same rigor as the standing daily prep sheet.
